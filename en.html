About us

We are two friends brought together by years of work in the lingerie trade - and by a shared dream of opening a shop where every woman feels comfortable, confident and welcome.

We had a great deal of support along the way. The partner of one of us has worked in lingerie retail for many years, and thanks to his experience, his advice and the backing he gave the whole project, our idea became real.

We have put everything we learned over those years into Láska v krajkách. We believe the right lingerie is not only a matter of size or cut, but above all of how it makes you feel: comfortable, confident, and glad to be beautiful as you are.

We choose quality brands carefully and give every customer our full attention. We will gladly help you find something that fits perfectly and feels wonderful to wear.

We want a visit to Láska v krajkách to be more than a purchase - a pleasant experience, and a place you will be happy to come back to.

We look forward to your visit.

Sizing guide

A good fit starts with the right number

All you need is a tape measure and two measurements. For the underbust, measure snugly just below the bust, keeping the tape level and pulled firm. For the bust, measure around the fullest point, loosely, without compressing.

The calculator works out your European size and suggests sister sizes worth trying. Treat the result as a starting point - every brand cuts a little differently, which is why we always try things on here.

-

Band = your underbust rounded to the nearest 5 cm (73–77 cm → 75, 78–82 cm → 80…).

Cup = bust minus the band number. Every two centimetres of difference is one cup size up.
